# SlotDrop — Startup Buildathon India 2026 Pitch Blueprint

> **"Doorstep parcel deliveries on your schedule, not when the universe decides."**

---

## 1. The Core Indian Problem Statement
- **The "Arriving Today" Paradox:** High-speed quick commerce delivers groceries in 10 minutes, but ₹800–₹5,000 fashion & electronics parcels arrive with zero time predictability.
- **The Workday Conflict:** Delivery agents repeatedly call residents during office meetings while they are 15–25 km away in tech parks.
- **Gate Heap of Shame:** In gated communities (500–2,500 apartments), 150+ parcels are dumped daily in front of the security guard's desk, causing damage, monsoon water leaks, disputes, and theft.
- **Why MyGate / NoBrokerHood don't solve it:** They only handle gate entry approval ("Allow courier inside?"), with zero chain-of-custody, vaulting, or scheduled delivery.

---

## 2. The Solution: SlotDrop
SlotDrop is the **scheduled last-100-meters concierge** for high-rise gated communities in India.

1. **Intake & AI Digitization:** Courier partners (Amazon, Flipkart, Delhivery, BlueDart) dump all community parcels at the SlotDrop Ground Hub in 5 minutes.
2. **Instant Vault Notification:** Resident receives a ping: *"Your 2 parcels are safely vaulted in Bin B-14."*
3. **Scheduled Doorstep Batch:** Resident opens the SlotDrop app and picks their time:
   - *Tonight 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM*
   - *Tonight 9:30 PM – 10:30 PM*
   - *Tomorrow Morning 7:30 AM – 8:30 AM*
   - *Weekend Consolidated Batch (Saturday 11 AM)*
4. **Secure Handover:** Society runner delivers all day's parcels in 1 elevator trip with a 4-digit OTP handover.
